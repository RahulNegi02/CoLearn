Frequently Asked Support Questions

Quick answers to common questions about enrollment, simulations, and apprenticeships.

How do I access the 360Â° Factory Simulations?

All enrolled learners receive direct web-based access to our Hydroelectric Dam, Wind Turbine, and Solar Farm simulations. They run smoothly inside any modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) without requiring VR goggles or standalone software downloads.

Are CoLearn certifications officially recognized?

Yes. All courses culminate in an NCCT (National Centre for Construction and Technology) verified certificate, aligned with UK Level 3 and 4 national occupational standards and trusted by leading engineering contractors and green-energy utilities.

How does career matching and placement work?

Once you finish core theory and simulation competencies, your profile is unlocked in the CoLearn Employer Network. Our career advisors match you with vetted apprenticeship vacancies and arrange interview schedules across the UK.

Is financial assistance or funding available?

Eligible UK residents over 19 can access government-subsidized Skills Bootcamp funding covering up to 100% of course fees. Additionally, many of our corporate partner employers offer sponsored training bursaries.
