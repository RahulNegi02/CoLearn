Vocational Careers & Apprenticeship FAQs

Everything you need to know about embarking on a technical vocational career with CoLearn.

Do I need previous industry experience or GCSE qualifications?

Most Level 3 apprenticeship pathways require GCSE English and Maths at Grade 4/C or equivalent Functional Skills. Prior vocational experience is an advantage but not required — our programs start with foundational concepts.

Do I get paid while training on a CoLearn apprenticeship?

Yes. All registered UK apprentices receive a contracted monthly salary from day one, plus statutory holiday entitlement, workplace pension contributions, and protected training hours.

How do simulations benefit my job applications?

Hiring managers in technical sectors value hands-on familiarization. Our 360° simulations allow you to demonstrate working knowledge of turbine internals, dam hydraulics, and high-voltage safety during interviews.

Can I get guidance on choosing the right career track?

Absolutely. You can discuss your goals with a vocational advisor on our Support & Advisory page or chat with our 24/7 AI Course Assistant anytime.

Looking for bespoke advice?

Our career advisors connect NCCT learners directly with verified industry employers across construction, renewable energy, and advanced manufacturing.
