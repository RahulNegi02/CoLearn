Are these courses free for registered learners?

Yes. All NCCT foundational and vocational pathways on CoLearn are funded through vocational training partnerships for eligible UK residents, trainees, and apprentices.

How do I claim my verified certificate?

Once you finish all course modules and achieve 80% or higher on the knowledge assessment, your verified Certificate ID is instantly generated with a secure PDF download.

Can I access learning on mobile or tablet?

How CoLearn works

CoLearn brings NCCT learning into a clear, practical course journey.

Each course combines short video lessons, guided practice and checks for understanding. Learners can follow progress module by module and return to resources whenever they need them.

Explore site safety, machinery operation and digital skills pathways with realistic examples, downloadable guidance and assessments.

Demo courses are available to browse and enrol in locally. Learning progress is saved in this browser while backend services are unavailable.

Enquire about this programme

Who is CoLearn for?

For learners building a foundation in construction, engineering and manufacturing.

Start with foundational learning or continue into more specialised machinery and digital courses.

Structured modules support independent learning, while attendance and progress views help learners and tutors stay aligned.

Course data is realistic demo content. Enrolment, progress and saved opportunities are stored locally in this browser.
